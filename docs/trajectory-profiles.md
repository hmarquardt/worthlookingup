# Historical ascent profiles: provenance and validation

Generated by `scripts/build-trajectory-profiles.mjs` from `data/launches/trajectory-profiles.json`;
edit the script, never this file. Every profile below passed **both** anchoring gates. Candidates
that failed a gate are listed with the numbers that failed them and are not published as trajectories.

Gate tolerances: plane consistency within 3 degrees of the achieved inclination,
and clearance of the neglected corrections (earth rotation (relative vs inertial azimuth); post-liftoff yaw steering or dogleg); terminal state within 3 per cent of the vis-viva speed at the reconstructed geocentric radius and within 25 km of the nearer apsis. The tolerances are justified by the gap between the measured passing population (within 1.3 per cent and 5 km) and the failing one (at least 4.8 per cent in speed, or 429 km and beyond in altitude).

## What is deliberately not here

- No brightness, apparent plume size or probability of sighting: none of that is derivable from these sources.
- No trajectory for any future launch: a reconstruction of a past flight is not a prediction, and no profile is
  attached to an upcoming launch by rocket family or launch site.
- No hazard polygon treated as a flight path, and no synthetic fixture presented as a historical flight.
