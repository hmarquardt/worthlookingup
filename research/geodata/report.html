Availability and redistribution are separate facts. A source is production-eligible only when its licence permits commercial use and redistribution and it can be read by an automated job. The gate is computed from these fields, not typed in by hand.

Method and limits

  • Astronomy: the site's own Milky Way engine (unchanged) gives the core's altitude and azimuth on the target night.
  • Terrain: USGS 3DEP bare-earth elevation, 1024×1024 pixels over a 32 km box (about 31 m), bilinear sampling every 60 m, Earth curvature and standard refraction, eye height 1.6 m above the ground at the point.
  • Terrain does not model trees, crops, buildings, levees or vehicles. A low modelled horizon is not a clear view.
  • Access comes from USGS PAD-US ownership records plus the managing agency's own published statements, quoted with their URLs and the date read. PAD-US's public-access flag is not treated as permission.
  • Sky brightness is unavailable for every candidate. Upward radiance is not sky brightness, and no licensed, machine-readable sky-brightness dataset was obtainable without an account or a non-commercial licence.
  • Distances are great-circle on a sphere. No road distance, drive time or routing is computed.
